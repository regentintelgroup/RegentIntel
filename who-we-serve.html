    Who We Serve

    We extend your program — or become your security function entirely.

    Every engagement falls into one of two models: organizations with an existing security function that need specialized capability, and those that need a Security Professional to fill the role from the ground up.

    Extending Your Program

    You have a security function. You need specialized capability.

    Your CSO or security director owns the ESRM relationship internally. Regent Intel plugs into your existing governance structure, reports through your chain, and delivers intelligence, investigative, and protective capability that extends your program without duplicating your role.

    Security Leaders

    CSOs & Security Directors

    ESRM-aligned capability to extend your enterprise security program. Intelligence, investigations, TSCM, and protective operations that integrate with your risk framework and report through your governance structure.

    Risk Committees

    Board-Level Security Governance

    Independent security risk assessments, threat intelligence briefings, and governance-grade reporting structured for board presentation, audit committee review, and enterprise risk oversight.

    Corporate Counsel

    Executive & Corporate Risk

    IRC §132 security assessments, executive threat analysis, due diligence on partners and key personnel, and corporate intelligence programs. Structured for board-level governance and legal review.

    Investigative Agencies

    Overflow & Specialized Capacity

    Available to local and regional PI agencies requiring intelligence-grade methodology, national reach, or specialized capability beyond their current capacity. All referrals handled confidentially.

    Becoming Your Security Professional

    You don't have a security function. You need one.

    Principals, family offices, executives, and smaller organizations without a dedicated security function. Regent Intel fills the entire Security Professional role: we identify the assets that matter, assess the risks, present mitigation options, and execute the program you approve. You make the risk decisions. We do everything else.

    Principals

    A Shifting Threat Picture

    A wealth event, a lawsuit, a public role, or escalating attention. The exposure is new, or newly serious, and generic coverage no longer fits. We apply the full ESRM cycle to your specific risk environment.

    Family Offices

    Protecting More Than a Principal

    Estates, travel, family members, and the reputational surface area that comes with all of it. One standard applied across every environment, one risk management framework governing every decision.

    Executives

    Who Cannot Afford to Look Protected

    Leadership visibility, travel, and public activity handled so the protection reads as staff, not security. Risk mitigation designed for executives whose roles demand normal movement and accessible presence.

    Advisors & Counsel

    Acting on Behalf of Someone Else

    Attorneys, fiduciaries, and family office leaders who need a security risk management partner that briefs clearly, produces governance-grade deliverables, and handles the principal discreetly.

    Professional Partners

    Your client. Your engagement. Our capability.

    Regent Intel is available to attorneys, family offices, corporate counsel, and local investigative agencies as a licensed investigative and protective partner. We operate within the scope of your engagement, under your direction, and produce findings structured for your specific legal and advisory purpose.

    We do not contact your client directly without your authorization. All referral engagements are scoped, structured, and delivered to your specifications. Findings are produced in whatever format your engagement requires: legal brief, advisory memo, or evidentiary exhibit.
