    Training & Certification

    Training by people who built the tradecraft.

    Certification programs developed and instructed by practitioners with backgrounds in the U.S. Marine Corps, U.S. Army Counterintelligence & Special Operations, and OGA Global Response Staff. Not a generic curriculum. The same methodology that governs Regent Intel operations governs these certifications.

    Certification Programs

    Four certifications. One standard.

    Each program is self-contained, practitioner-level, and built for working professionals. Courses are delivered through our learning portal. Enrollment is by application. All certifications are issued under Regent Intelligence Group and carry the RIG credential designation.

    PIA-C RIG-PIA-C-001 · 5.5 Hours · 80% Threshold

    Protective Intelligence Analyst Certification

    Pull your last report. Read it like a subpoena: because one day someone will.

    Built for protective intelligence analysts, threat analysts, security managers, and licensed investigators who perform intelligence functions. If you collect information about people for protective purposes, this certification applies to you. It protects you, your employer, and your client from legal exposure.

    Five modules. Graded assessments. 80% threshold throughout. Each module must be passed before the next opens.

    Module 1 The intelligence/investigation boundary. Threshold Determination and Three Tests. You cannot work this discipline without understanding this line.
    Module 2 Legal framework. FCRA, state PI licensing, privacy law, ECPA. The specific statutes and where they bite.
    Module 3 Collection program construction. Framing statement through source register to delivered assessment. PATHS scoring and threat assessment structure.
    Module 4 Handoff protocol. Seven triggers that convert an intelligence task to an investigative task. Concurrent action framework and parallel track architecture.
    Module 5 Work product. Executive briefings, written assessments, defensibility standards, discoverability, organizational liability.

    You leave with the ability to build a legally defensible collection program, score a threat, authorize a predicate, execute a handoff, and produce work product that can stand up to legal scrutiny. PIA-C is the prerequisite for CMI-C.

    CMI-C RIG-CMI-C-001 · 80% Threshold · Prerequisite: PIA-C

    Competitive/Market Intelligence Practitioner Certification

    Built for analysts and practitioners working in corporate intelligence, competitive research, market intelligence, and opposition research environments. CMI-C covers a separate discipline with a separate legal framework: collection oriented toward a subject who has not triggered the engagement, where the legal framework alone defines what is permissible.

    Six content modules plus orientation. 80% threshold. PIA-C is required first.

    Introduction The CMI discipline and its distinction from protective intelligence and formal investigation.
    Module 1 Predicate authorization and engagement scoping.
    Module 2 Legal framework specific to commercial intelligence work.
    Module 3 Collection methodology and source discipline in the CMI context.
    Module 4 Work product standards and defensibility requirements.
    Module 5 The CMI/investigation boundary and handoff protocol.
    Module 6 Capstone. Full engagement scenario scored at 80%.
    OIPT-C RIG-OIPT-C-001 · 13 Hours · 13 CEUs · 75% Threshold

    Operative Intelligence & Protective Tradecraft Certification

    Built for security operators, executive protection agents, security managers, and professionals with backgrounds in the GRS community or equivalent operational environments. A signal course for the GRS professional community specifically: the doctrine, framing, and operational scenarios are calibrated for an audience that has operated in high-threat environments.

    Eight modules. 75% threshold: not a lower standard, a different standard for a different discipline. Operational judgment under pressure cannot be assessed the same way legal compliance is.

    Module 1 Intelligence-led security operations. What it means to build a security program on intelligence rather than reaction.
    Module 2 Surveillance detection and countersurveillance. Behavioral and environmental indicators, SDR construction, countersurveillance program design.
    Module 3 Protective intelligence in operational context. Threat assessment driving protective posture. PATHS scoring from the operator's perspective.
    Module 4 Source development. Identifying, developing, and managing sources in a protective intelligence context. Operational and legal boundaries. Three Tests applied to HUMINT.
    Module 5 Investigative tradecraft and the intelligence handoff. Seven triggers. PAF process. The handoff protocol from the operator's side.
    Module 6 Hostile environment operations. Threat recognition, movement security, pattern of life discipline, and communication security in ambient threat environments.
    Module 7 Insider threat detection. Behavioral baseline, deviation recognition, and the distinction between insider threat intelligence and investigation.
    Module 8 Security program design. Building a security program on an intelligence foundation. Threat environment assessment, resource allocation, SOPs, and program evaluation.

    OIPT-C is a standalone credential. PIA-C is not required: but if you hold it, the Three Tests and predicate authorization framework apply here and you will recognize them. Standalone or combined, the operational standard is the same.
