    Training & Certification

    Training by people who built the tradecraft.

    Certification programs developed and instructed by senior practitioners drawn from OGA programs, Army intelligence, special operations, federal financial investigations, and federal law enforcement. Not adapted from a generic curriculum. The same methodology that governs Regent Intel operations governs these certifications.

    Certification Programs

    Four certifications. One standard.

    Each program is self-contained, practitioner-level, and built for working professionals. Courses are delivered through our learning portal. Enrollment is by application.

    PIA-C

    Pull your last report. Read it like a subpoena — because one day someone will.

    Protective Intelligence Analyst Certification

    Built for protective intelligence analysts, threat analysts, security managers, and licensed investigators who perform intelligence functions. If you collect information about people for protective purposes, this certification applies to you. It protects you, your employer, and your client from legal exposure.

    Five modules. Graded assessments. 80% threshold throughout.

    Module 1 The intelligence/investigation boundary. Threshold Determination and Three Tests.
    Module 2 Legal framework. FCRA, state PI licensing, privacy law, ECPA. Where the statutes bite.
    Module 3 Collection program construction. Framing statement through source register to delivered assessment. PATHS scoring.
    Module 4 Handoff protocol. Seven triggers that convert an intelligence task to an investigative task. Concurrent action and parallel track architecture.
    Module 5 Work product. Executive briefings, written assessments, defensibility standards, discoverability, organizational liability.

    You leave with the ability to build a legally defensible collection program, score a threat, authorize a predicate, execute a handoff, and produce work product that can stand up to legal scrutiny. PIA-C is the prerequisite for CMI-C.

    OIPT-C

    Operative Intelligence & Protective Tradecraft Certification

    Built for security operators, executive protection agents, and security managers with operational responsibilities. 13 hours / 13 CEUs. Foundational tradecraft for operators working at the intersection of protective intelligence and physical security — built from the same doctrine that governs Regent Intel field operations.

    CMI-C

    Competitive/Market Intelligence Certification

    Built for analysts and practitioners working in corporate intelligence, competitive research, and market intelligence environments. Practitioner-level training in collection, analysis, and reporting aligned with Federal and state regulatory requirements. PIA-C is the prerequisite.

    AEP-C

    Advanced Executive Protection Certification

    For experienced EP practitioners only. Prerequisite: documented field experience. Application required. 15-day resident course covering extreme firearms proficiency: close-quarters, vehicle-borne, low-light response, medical readiness, and integrated counter-surveillance methodology, taught to Regent Intel standard at a dedicated training location.

    Enrollment by application.
