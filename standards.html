    Standards

    Three disciplines. One standard of accountability.

    Confidentiality

    Practitioner Standards

    Selected for judgment. Not resume volume.

    Practitioners operating within Regent Intel programs are selected through a rigorous vetting process that evaluates more than operational capability. Judgment, maturity, and discretion are the primary selection criteria: the qualities essential to operating within private environments where professionalism and restraint are paramount.

    Regent Intel draws practitioners from OGA programs, Army intelligence, special operations, federal financial investigations, and federal law enforcement. The integration of those disciplines: not the depth of any single one: is what the methodology reflects and what no comparable firm has replicated.

    Prior credentials do not exempt anyone from the firm's internal validation process. The standard applies consistently, to everyone, on every engagement.
