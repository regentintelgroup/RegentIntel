9.1 The DHS Tank Gauge Bulletin

[E-MODERATE] The bulletin's operative finding is that Iranian cyber actors researched US storage tank capacity in early August using a commercial model, that the research was likely directed at the industrial control systems of a US energy company operating oil wells, and that Iran likely intends to target automated tank gauges. The effects it lists run from false readings to pump shutoff and suppressed overheating alerts. DHS distributed it to energy companies and law enforcement so they can take defensive measures (S-001, S-002, S-003).

ANALYST ASSESSMENT: Attribution rung reached: 5 (state sponsorship) as stated by DHS. Three points bound the significance. First, the bulletin describes research, not intrusion; the only confirmed gauge intrusions of 2026 are May's, and they were not attributed. Second, tank gauges are the most exposed class of OT in the US fuel chain and have been for a decade, so the targeting requires no special access. Third, the stated effects are local and reversible, which fits Iran's pattern in water: visible disruption with reputational and psychological effect rather than physical destruction. Guidance for this vector says take gauges off the internet rather than naming a targeted company, because the rung reached on independent evidence is 5 and no company is named.
