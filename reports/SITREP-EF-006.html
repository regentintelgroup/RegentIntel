CNN could not verify or geolocate the footage; its analyst Cedric Leighton stated the markings are consistent with a Saudi F-15SA. Saudi Arabia has not commented. (S-001, S-003, S-002).

9.2 September 16: The President on the War's End and a Direct Channel

[E-MODERATE] Speaking on the tarmac in Charlotte, the President stated the war is hopefully toward the end, that Iran wants to make a deal, and that Washington deals directly rather than through intermediaries. Tehran's public line the same day was Rezaei's no-talks position and Araghchi's statement in Beijing that Iran has agreed with Oman on a plan to reopen Hormuz. No Iranian official confirmed a direct channel (S-005, S-022).

ANALYST ASSESSMENT: Attribution rung reached: not applicable; the item is a leadership statement. Three hypotheses were assessed: H1, a direct channel exists at working level and Tehran denies it publicly; H2, the President refers to the Oman corridor talks; H3, no channel exists and the statement is positioning before the General Assembly. H2 is least contradicted by available evidence and is carried as the working assessment (likely, low confidence): the Oman plan is the only negotiation any party has acknowledged this week. H1 is assessed roughly even chance. H3 is assessed unlikely. The single piece of evidence that would most change this assessment is an Iranian official confirming or denying direct contact by name.

9.3 September 16: Houthi Video of a Downed Saudi F-15

[E-MODERATE] The Houthi military media wing released footage of a missile strike on a jet and of fighters at a crash site with a tailfin bearing Saudi insignia and the number 5539, chanting; Saree stated the aircraft was downed over Marib with a locally made missile and did not give a date. Saudi authorities have not acknowledged any loss. The claimed area is about 75 miles east of Sanaa (S-001, S-002, S-003).

ANALYST ASSESSMENT: Attribution rung reached: 5 (organizational sponsorship) for the Houthi claim as the movement's own statement; the loss itself is unverified. If confirmed, the kill would be the second F-15 lost to hostile fire in this war after the US F-15E downed by Iran, and the first coalition fast-jet loss to Houthi ground fire; Houthi surface-to-air systems have previously downed coalition drones and, in 2015 to 2022, at least one Saudi F-15 by Houthi claim without Saudi confirmation, so the pattern count is one confirmed and two claimed, an observation and not a pattern.

9.4 September 15-16: The Red Sea Export Route and the Sohar Workaround

[E-MODERATE] Sources told Reuters on September 16 that Saudi Arabia is offering Asian refiners additional crude by ship-to-ship transfer off Sohar, Oman, which lies outside Hormuz; Yanbu loadings remain suspended and late-September European cargoes cancelled. Satellite analysts see heavy damage at one pumping station. Poland's Orlen is tendering for non-Saudi grades (S-015, S-018, S-013).

ANALYST ASSESSMENT: The evidence chain runs: OBSERVED, no Saudi crude has left Yanbu since September 11 and a pumping station shows heavy damage on imagery (S-018, S-013); KNOWN, the East-West line carried Saudi crude to the Red Sea at up to 7 million barrels a day capacity while Hormuz was constrained; HISTORICAL, the 2019 Abqaiq repair took weeks with Aramco publishing a timeline within days, which it has not done here; PATTERN, three outlets now place the outage in weeks against one official in days; INFERENCE, Riyadh is treating the outage as weeks, which the Sohar offer and the escorted Hormuz transits show; HIGHER-ORDER INFERENCE, Saudi export capacity for the rest of September is Hormuz-plus-Sohar, which returns the kingdom's revenue to the waterway the war is about.
