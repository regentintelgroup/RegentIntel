[E-MODERATE] Three people told Reuters that the President told senior advisers he may hold off on attacking Iran if Tehran and the Houthis make no new provocations. [E-MODERATE] Iran refuses to discuss its nuclear program until Washington accepts its ceasefire conditions, according to the Associated Press. Qatar's push for an interim deal to restore Hormuz shipping is on hold. The President posted that the United States does not need a deal with Iran, and the Mossad director was in Washington for talks on the war. (S-005, S-009, S-010)

The officials' own intelligence that strikes would not move Tehran's demands weakens the case for resuming them, but the November 13 drone launch already tests the no-provocation condition. A U.S.-acknowledged strike on Iranian territory or waters conducted between November 12 and November 17 is unlikely (EF-060-P1). A U.S. statement answering the Bahrain launch would change this.

The IRGC statement stopped short of naming the United States, and CENTCOM did not mention Sirik. (S-012, S-013, S-014)

Reuters could not verify the claims, and none of Iran's recent mine claims has been independently confirmed. (S-016, S-017, S-018)

Three vessels continued their voyage, three needed assistance, two outcomes were unknown, and none sank. Transits fell to 308, from 325 the week before, about 65 percent below pre-war levels, and UKMTO has logged 163 conflict incidents since February 28. (S-019, S-020, S-022)

9.1 November 12 to 13: Sirik Tower Struck; IRGC Drones Downed Near Bahrain

[E-MODERATE] An air attack hit a telecommunications tower in Sirik, Hormozgan province, which hosts part of Iran's radar and communications network over the strait. The IRGC then launched drones toward Bahrain. CENTCOM reported both destroyed and called the IRGC radar claim false. (S-014, S-012)

9.2 November 13: Brent Ends the Week Down as the IEA Sees a Surplus

[E-MODERATE] Brent settled at $95.84, up 2 cents, and fell about 2.5 percent on the week; WTI settled at $88.56. The International Energy Agency said the market will be in surplus this quarter for the first time since the war began. Prices briefly rose on reports that the Mossad director was in Washington. (S-007, S-008, S-010)

9.3 November 14: Iran Claims Three Tankers Hit Mines

[E-LOW] The claims came in two statements carried by Iranian state media, one from the navy and one from the IRGC Navy. Neither named a vessel. The IRGC said on November 13 that a liquefied gas carrier had also hit a mine. (S-016, S-017)

9.4 November 14: UKMTO Weekly Overview and UN Call for Safe Passage

[E-MODERATE] UKMTO rated the strait threat as severe. At the Security Council, UN Assistant Secretary-General Khiari called for an immediate end to attacks on civilian ships. The Quad leaders are expected to discuss commercial shipping security in Manila on November 17. (S-019, S-028, S-027)

9.5 November 14: Coalition Intercepts Two Houthi Drones

[E-MODERATE] Coalition spokesman al-Maliki said the drones were destroyed over the Riyadh region and the south. The coalition has reported Houthi intercepts on most days since November 1. (S-023, S-024)

9.6 November 14: Shenzhen Meeting Set; Rubio Leaves Room for a Deal

[E-MODERATE] U.S. officials said the President will raise Iran, not Ukraine, at a meeting with Xi and Putin in Shenzhen on November 19. Rubio said economic pressure has weakened Tehran and reduced the need for further strikes. (S-025, S-026, S-032)
