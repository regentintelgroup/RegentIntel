7.1 Iran Fires on Four Days in Six; Washington Answers at a Launch Site

[E-MODERATE] CENTCOM shot down two IRGC drones launched toward Bahrain on November 13, the first Iranian attack on a Gulf state since early September. On November 15 the IRGC said six drones hit Riffa Air Base; Bahrain said it intercepted the attacks, and CENTCOM reported no impact on U.S. forces. On November 17 CENTCOM said drones aimed at a U.S.-operated radar at Sheikh Isa Air Base were intercepted without damage. (S-009, S-006, S-007, S-008, S-005)

[E-MODERATE] Hours later, U.S. fighters struck an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island in self-defense, according to an unnamed U.S. military official. No named official, release, or presidential post acknowledged the strike. Kuwait reported hostile drone attacks that night, and Bahrain reported Iranian aerial attacks on the morning of November 18. CENTCOM said no U.S. personnel were injured in any attempted attack that week. (S-001, S-002, S-011, S-013, S-014)

Attribution rung reached: 6 (state direction) for the attacks on Bahrain and Kuwait, which the IRGC and Iran's army claim as their own operations.

ANALYST ASSESSMENT: Iran has moved from silence to answering each U.S. action at bases in the two nearest host states, with salvos small enough for air defenses to absorb. Washington has answered once, at a launch site, and has kept its name off the strike. A further Iranian launch at a U.S.-hosting base between November 19 and 21 is very likely (EF-061-P1). A full day with no launch would change this.
