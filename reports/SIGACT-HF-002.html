Evidence chain. OBSERVED: water PLC incidents in at least seven states since July 27, some with operational effect (S-003). KNOWN: CISA's April advisory named Iran-affiliated actors exploiting the same device class; the July update added vendors and project-file theft (S-005). HISTORICAL: the 2023 Unitronics campaign was attributed to IRGC-CEC-affiliated actors and produced Treasury designations. PATTERN: three campaigns against the same device class in three years.

INFERENCE: the September telecom and energy targeting is conducted by the same actor set (likely, moderate confidence). HIGHER-ORDER INFERENCE: Tehran is holding cyber effects in reserve rather than lacking capability (roughly even chance, low confidence). Attribution rung reached: 4 (organizational association).

ANALYST ASSESSMENT: The report does not change the HIGH rating and does not raise it, because a widened but unsuccessful campaign is the same rung and the same effect as before. It changes guidance: telecommunications and energy operators now carry the same PLC-exposure instruction water utilities received in July.
