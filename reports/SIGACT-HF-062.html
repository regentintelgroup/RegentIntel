7.2 An Indictment Details a Racially Motivated Plot Against an Aurora Temple

[E-MODERATE] A Cook County grand jury indicted Alexandros Paporakis, 19, of Palatine, Illinois, on November 5 on six counts of terrorism, two of armed violence, two of attempted terrorism, and nine of making a terrorist threat. Police say he drove into the lot of a Hindu temple in Aurora on October 11, the first day of Navaratri, and left after his gun jammed. (S-001, S-002)

[E-MODERATE] Prosecutors say he calls himself a white supremacist and neo-Nazi and told co-workers he planned to kill Indian people; the co-workers reported him and police arrested him that afternoon. He is detained, and his attorney says he will plead not guilty. The FBI's Chicago office had no information on possible federal charges. (S-001, S-003, S-004)

Attribution rung reached: 1 (activity observed); ideology as stated by prosecutors; no organizational association is alleged.

ANALYST ASSESSMENT: The case confirms the threat that keeps this vector at ELEVATED: a lone actor with a stated racial motive choosing a faith site on a festival day. It does not move the rating, because the attempt failed before anyone was hurt and the indictment as reported names no accomplice or further target. A federal charge against the suspect by December 6 is very unlikely (HF-062-P2). An accomplice or a second plan in the charging papers would change this.
